In the story, a group of highly intelligent beings builds a supercomputer called **Deep Thought** to calculate **“the Answer to the Ultimate Question of Life, the Universe, and Everything.”**

After **7.5 million years**, Deep Thought finally announces the answer: **42**.

Everyone is baffled. What does that mean? Deep Thought explains that the problem is **they never really knew what question they were asking.**

So it designs an even larger computer to discover the “Ultimate Question” whose answer is 42. That computer is **Earth**, and life on Earth is part of the calculation.

But just before the calculation is complete, aliens demolish Earth to make way for a hyperspace bypass.

The joke is that **we think a precise answer will help us understand everything, but the real challenge may be knowing what we’re actually asking.**
